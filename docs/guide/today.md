# Today

The Today page is where Aplyd opens. It shows what happens at a set time today, what is due, and the drafts waiting for your decision. A short summary of yesterday sits at the bottom.

## The number on the Today tab

The badge next to **Today** in the sidebar counts three things added together:

1. Drafts waiting for you to approve.
2. Action items due by the end of today, or already overdue.
3. Interviews booked at a set time today.

When all three are zero the badge is gone and the page reads "All caught up."

## The day strip and the lists

- A strip across the top shows anything that happens at a set time today, such as an interview or a call. Click one to see it in full.
- Below that, items are split into **Coming up** (dated later today), **Anytime**, **Cover letters to read**, **Practice** and **Going quiet**.
- Each item has a checkbox. Click it to tick the item off, or open the item and click **Mark done**. A done item drops off Today.
- Open an item to see its notes and the buttons for it. **Open email** opens the matching message in your browser. A link button opens the job listing. **Open** takes you to the application.

## Yesterday's numbers

A line at the bottom of the page shows four counts for yesterday: applications you added (**applied yesterday**), **job replies**, **Outreach sent** and **Outreach replies**. If an agent has logged a run, the line also shows **Last agent run** with the agent's name, when it started and its summary, or "Still running." if it has not finished.

## Drafts to approve

When your agent has written an email or outreach draft, it lands under **Drafts to approve**. Open a draft to read the full subject, recipient and body.

Each draft gives you three choices:

- **Approve** marks it ready to send.
- **Edit** opens the draft so you can change it before approving.
- **Skip** sets it aside. It stays in the history.

To clear a whole pile at once, use **Approve all** in the section heading.

## How a draft is sent

Aplyd writes drafts. It does not send them. The line under every draft says it plainly:

> Aplyd never sends. Your agent sends approved drafts.

After you approve a draft it waits for your agent. Your agent (Codex or Claude Code, running the Aplyd skills in your own session) picks up approved drafts on its next run and sends them from your own account. Approving in Aplyd only records your decision. See [Agent skills in Settings](settings.md) and [the skill guide](../../skills/README.md) for how the agent connects.
