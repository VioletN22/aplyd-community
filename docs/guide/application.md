# The application page

Open any row from [Jobs](jobs.md) to see one application in full: its stage, fit, cover letter, interview prep, emails and notes.

## Header and stage bar

- The header shows the role, company, location and source.
- The stage bar moves the application through the funnel: Applied, Assessment, Assessed, Phone screen, Interview and Offer, with Rejected and Withdrawn as exits. Click a stage to move there.

## Fit score

- **Fit** shows a score out of 10 with the reason for it when you hover. The fit is scored once when the application is added.
- **Rescore** runs it again against your best-fit resume. This is useful after a resume variant has changed. If nothing has been scored yet the button reads **Score the fit**.

Fit scoring needs an AI provider. See [docs/ai-providers.md](../ai-providers.md).

## Resume sent

Under the header, **Sent:** shows which resume went with this application, with a small menu to change it. Your pick feeds the "What's working" by-resume table in [Stats](stats.md).

## Interview prep

The **Interview prep** area writes a one-page sheet for you: what the company does, likely questions, and stories from your resumes that answer them. It appears once the application reaches a phone screen or interview stage, or once a sheet exists.

- **Regenerate** rebuilds the sheet. If a saved prep file already exists, the button offers a short version instead ("Write a short version").
- **Edit** turns the sheet into plain text so you can add your own notes.

Interview prep needs an AI provider.

## Assistant

The **Assistant** holds this posting, your resumes and your letters, so you do not paste anything in. Drop in a question a form is asking, and it answers in your voice, ready to paste. It can also help with "Why am I a fit here?" and "Draft a follow-up message." The Assistant needs an AI provider.

## Cover letter

The **Cover letter** area drafts a letter from everything Aplyd knows about you plus live research on the company and this posting.

- The draft auto-saves as you edit. It also writes to a PDF folder when you save a version.
- The refine box ("Tell me what to change") takes plain feedback, such as "shorter, lead with the React work, warmer tone." Press Enter to send, or click **Refine**.
- Tick **Remember as a style rule** to turn one piece of feedback into a rule every future letter follows. It is off by default, so feedback is a one-off edit unless you say otherwise.
- **Copy letter** copies the whole letter to your clipboard.
- **Save version + PDF** keeps a labelled copy in Aplyd and writes a PDF. Saved versions are listed below, where you can **Load** or delete them.
- **Regenerate** drafts a fresh letter.
- The **PDF folder** footer shows where PDFs go, with **Change** to pick a different folder and **Open** to open it.

If the application was added with just company and role, paste the posting here first: there is nothing to write a letter from yet. The cover letter needs an AI provider.

## Emails, interview, location, source and notes

- The **Emails** panel lists messages linked to this application. It stays hidden when there are none.
- Set an interview date and time, and a duration in minutes, in the sidebar. Set the date and the row shows it on the list.
- The sidebar also holds the workplace location, the source dropdown, the stage **History** and a free notes box. Notes save when you click away.
