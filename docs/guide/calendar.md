# Calendar and Upcoming

Aplyd keeps your interviews, deadlines, outreach calls and follow-ups in one place. The local calendar works before you connect anything.

## Upcoming

The **Upcoming** page lists interviews, to-dos and commitments for the next two weeks, day by day, then the rest of what is dated, grouped by month. The sidebar count next to **Upcoming** is the number of items in the next 7 days.

- Each timed item shows its time. Each to-do shows a checkbox.
- Click the checkbox to mark a to-do done.
- A to-do that is over two weeks late shows a **Dismiss** button, so you can clear it if you no longer want it.
- "Open the calendar" at the top takes you to the full calendar.

## The Calendar page

The calendar has three controls:

- **Week** shows a timed week grid.
- **Month** shows the whole month at a glance.
- **Today** jumps back to the current week or month.

Click an event to open a popover with its full time and a button to open the matching application, lead or task. A to-do in the calendar also has a checkbox, and a **Dismiss** button once it is over two weeks late.

## The legend

The legend names the colours: Interviews, To do, Shifts, Outreach, Commitment and Google Calendar. The legend only shows entries that apply. Interviews, To do, Outreach and Google Calendar are always shown; the others appear only when you have items of that kind.

## Local calendar preferences

Open **Settings**, then the **Calendar** section, to set your local preferences.

1. Set your **Timezone** using its IANA name (for example `America/New_York` or `Europe/Berlin`).
2. Click **Use system timezone** to fill in your computer's timezone.
3. Click **Add weekly commitment** to add a repeating block (title, weekday, start and end time, an optional last day, and dates to skip).
4. Click **Save calendar preferences**.

You should now see your timezone and any weekly commitments reflected on the calendar.

## Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional and advanced. It takes about 20 minutes to set up a Google project the first time. The steps live in [docs/setup.md](../setup.md). Follow that guide rather than the short form in Settings.
