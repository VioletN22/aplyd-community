# Jobs

The Jobs page lists every application you are tracking. Use it to add applications, move them through stages, and find one fast.

## The views

Tabs across the top narrow the list by where each application is in the funnel. Each tab shows a count.

- **In play** shows applications that are moving: assessment, take-home, phone screen, interview and offer stages.
- **Waiting** shows applications you have applied to and are still waiting to hear back on.
- **No response** shows applications that have gone quiet. These are hidden from the other views, so they do not clutter the list.
- **Closed** shows rejected and withdrawn applications.
- **Everything** shows all applications except the No response pile.

## Finding and reading rows

- The search box matches on company, role, location, source, skills and notes. Every word you type must appear somewhere in the application.
- The density control switches the layout. **Table** (compact) is a dense grid with columns for Company, Role, Stage, Fit, Status, Source, Location and Applied. **Cards** (comfortable) is a roomier card per application. Your choice is remembered.
- Flags and pills on a row show the stage, the fit score and the location.
- Click a row to open its [application page](application.md).

## Adding applications

Click **Add job** at the top of the list, or **New job** at the bottom of the sidebar, to open the **Add application** modal. You can also press Cmd+N on macOS or Ctrl+N elsewhere. The modal has two tabs:

- **Paste posting or URL**: paste the whole job posting, or a link to it. This is the default. Aplyd reads the details onto a new application.
- **Just company + role**: log a row now with no posting attached. Paste the posting on its page later. Letters and the fit score need the real posting.

Other things in the modal:

1. On the **Paste posting or URL** tab, several links one per line add them all as a batch. Aplyd detects this and tells you how many postings it found. You can override it with "Add as one instead."
2. **Job source** is an optional dropdown for where you found the job. It lists LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Company website, Referral and Recruiter / Agency first, then Email, regional job boards (Seek, Prosple, GradConnection, Jora, CareerOne, Workforce Australia, Hatch) and Other. You cannot type your own source here. A source saved another way, for example by the daily applications skill, is kept and shown on the application page.
3. **Generate letter on add** (single adds only) starts the cover letter as soon as the application lands.
4. Press Cmd+Enter (or Ctrl+Enter) to submit from anywhere in the modal. Escape closes it.

A batch keeps running in the main process even if you close the modal. Rows appear in your list as they land.

You should now see the new application open on its own page, or new rows filling into the list as a batch completes.
