# Stats

The Stats page shows how your job search is going over a period you choose, with a dashboard and a breakdown of what is working.

## Picking a period

At the top, set **Review from** and **Through** to choose the date range. Click **Last 14 days** to reset to the default window. The numbers below reload for the range you pick.

## Jobs overview

The dashboard lives here, under the heading **Jobs overview**. It is the same dashboard of counts and drill-downs you reach from the sidebar. Click a card or row to open the matching applications in [Jobs](jobs.md).

## Period review

The **Period review** section compares the selected period against everything before it.

- **Jobs by role type** shows, per role type, how many applications you made, how many progressed (reached an assessment, phone screen, interview or offer), and how many were rejected. The last column shows the change in progress rate, in percentage points.
- **Leads by first contact** does the same for outreach, grouped by segment, showing response rates.
- A second table, **Leads, all recorded activity**, shows drafted, sent, replies, calls, won and response rate per segment.

Each table has notes under it explaining exactly how the numbers are counted.

## What's working

The **What's working** section shows the share of applications that progressed to an assessment, screen, interview or offer, broken down three ways: by job source, by resume sent, and by role family.

This section only appears once 10 applications have a reply or outcome. Until then it shows a single line with how many have an outcome so far. The best bucket in each column is highlighted.
