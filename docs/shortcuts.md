# Keyboard shortcuts

A short list of the keys Aplyd uses.

## In the app

- **Cmd+N** (macOS) or **Ctrl+N** (other systems): open the **Add application** modal. This is the **New application** item in the **File** menu.
- **Cmd+Enter** or **Ctrl+Enter**: submit the **Add application** modal from anywhere in it, or add a posting on the application page.
- **Enter**: send what you typed in the cover letter refine box on the application page. **Cmd+Enter** or **Ctrl+Enter** there does the same.
- **Escape**: close the open modal, popover or confirm dialog. In a confirm dialog, Escape is the same as **Cancel**.

## The Quick Add global shortcut

Quick Add works anywhere on your computer, not just inside Aplyd. Copy a job ad or link, then press the shortcut to add it.

- The default is **Control+Command+J** on macOS and **Control+Alt+J** on other systems.
- Change it in **Settings**, in the **Quick add** card, with the **Change** button.
- An empty shortcut switches Quick Add off.

If the shortcut is already taken by another app, Aplyd tells you and Quick Add stays off until you pick a free one.
