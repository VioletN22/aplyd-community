Graduate Software Engineer

Atlassian Sydney NSW

About the role

Join our graduate program and ship code that millions of teams use every day. You will rotate through two product teams over 12 months.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build and test features in Java and TypeScript
  • Participate in code reviews & incident retros
  • Pair with senior engineers on system design

Requirements

  • A degree in computer science or related field, completed in the last two years
  • Experience with at least one modern programming language

You will be supported by a dedicated mentor, a learning budget and regular feedback sessions, and you will present your work to the wider engineering group at the end of each rotation.

Full time, hybrid. Salary $95,000 plus super.
